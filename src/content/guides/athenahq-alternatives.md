AthenaHQ is a GEO platform: it tracks what AI models answer for your prompts, shows the sources and competitors in those answers, and has an agent that suggests content changes. It has a free plan, and the first paid plan is $295 a month.

Three things can send you looking elsewhere: the step from free to $295, credits that thin out quickly across eleven models, or needing one careful look rather than a monthly platform. Below is what AthenaHQ includes, what its credits buy, and six alternatives.

**A note on where we stand.** We make the first alternative on this list. What is written about AthenaHQ was read on its [plans page](https://athenahq.ai/plans) on 26 November 2026, and about the others on their own pages between 23 and 26 November. Prices change, so check the page before you buy.

## What AthenaHQ includes

| Plan | Price | Credits | Models |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Free | 300 | 5 |
| Starter | $295 a month | 3,600 a month | 11 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 11+ |

One credit is one AI response. The free plan covers ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot; the plans page does not say whether its 300 credits renew. Starter adds AI Mode, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI and Mistral.

Starter has no limit on topics, prompts, seats or competitors: the credits are the limit. Unused credits roll over for one month, and paying yearly takes 17% off. API access and extra credits are paid add-ons, and Starter tracks one language and region. Enterprise adds several regions and languages, single sign-on and a dedicated specialist.

## What 3,600 credits buy

Because one credit is one response, the credits divide by the number of models and by how often each prompt runs. If every prompt runs daily, 3,600 credits a month are 120 responses a day:

| Models per prompt | Prompts checked daily |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | 40 |
| 5 | 24 |
| All 11 | 10 |

**What one answer costs.** On Starter, a response costs about 8.2 cents. A check on OtterlyAI's Lite plan costs about 1.5 cents, and an answer on Peec AI's Starter plan about 2.1 cents. AthenaHQ's price also buys its content agent and recommendations, so the comparison is about tracking alone.

## 1. AI Visibility Report: one look, no subscription

This one is ours. The [free check](/ai-visibility-report) finds the searches your customers make and asks the most searched one on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The full report is $29, once. It asks five searches on six engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok twice each, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode once), about 50 answers, and shows every answer word for word, who is named in your place, the pages the answers were built from and a fix kit. The same searches are asked again after 30 days.

It is not a tracker and has no content agent, and it does not cover Perplexity or Copilot. **Pick it when** you want to know where you stand and what to fix before paying for a platform.

## 2. OtterlyAI: daily tracking from $29

[OtterlyAI's](https://otterly.ai/pricing) Lite plan is $29 a month for 2,000 credits, enough for 21 prompts checked daily on three engines. It covers seven engines, Claude among them at three credits a check, but not Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI or Mistral. Our guide to [OtterlyAI pricing](/guides/otterly-ai-pricing/) has its plans in full. **Pick it when** tracking a short list every day matters more than the number of models.

## 3. Peec AI: 50 prompts for a team

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) starts at $95 a month for 50 prompts checked daily on three models of your choice, with unlimited users. Claude and Grok are on its Enterprise plan only. **Pick it when** a marketing team needs a fixed set of 50 prompts and logs in every week.

## 4. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: inside Semrush

[Semrush's toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) is $99 a month per license, with 25 prompts checked daily and brand reports for one domain. Semrush says there is no free trial for it. **Pick it when** your keyword research already lives in Semrush.

## 5. Ahrefs Brand Radar: a whole market

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) sells Brand Radar from $199 a month. It measures how often brands are mentioned across a very large set of AI answers, beyond your own prompts. **Pick it when** you want to compare brands across a market rather than watch a list of prompts.

## 6. Profound: the enterprise option

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) sells one Enterprise plan at a custom price, after retiring its self-serve plans in September 2026. It tracks up to ten answer engines daily, with single sign-on, SOC 2 compliance and analytics of visits by AI agents. **Pick it when** you are a large brand comparing enterprise contracts.

## When AthenaHQ is the right choice

- **You need many models.** Eleven on Starter, including Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI and Mistral, is the widest list on a plan you can buy online among the tools here.
- **You want help with the content, not only the numbers.** The content agent and recommendations are part of the plan.
- **You want to try before paying.** The free plan covers five models and has no seat limit.

## Questions

### Is AthenaHQ free?

Its Essential plan is: 300 credits on ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot, with unlimited users. The first paid plan, Starter, is $295 a month.

### What is an AthenaHQ credit?

One AI response. A prompt asked on three models uses three credits each time it runs.

### Is AthenaHQ cheaper than OtterlyAI?

Not for tracking alone: a response costs about 8.2 cents on AthenaHQ's Starter plan against about 1.5 cents on OtterlyAI's Lite plan. AthenaHQ covers more models and includes a content agent, which is what the difference pays for.

### What is the cheapest AthenaHQ alternative?

For tracking, OtterlyAI's Lite plan at $29 a month. For a single look with the sources and the fixes, our report at $29 once.
