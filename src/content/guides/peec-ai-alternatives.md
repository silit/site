Peec AI is an AI visibility tracker for marketing teams. It asks a list of prompts every day on the AI models you choose and reports how often your brand appears in the answers. Its lowest plan is $95 a month.

Three things can send you looking elsewhere: the price, the limit of three models per plan, and models such as Claude and Grok that Peec offers only on its Enterprise plan. Below is what Peec includes, and six alternatives, with the reason to pick each one.

**A note on where we stand.** We make the first alternative on this list. What is written about Peec was read on its [pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing) on 25 November 2026, and about the others on their own pages between 23 and 25 November. Each one is linked. Prices change, so check the page before you buy.

## What Peec AI includes

| Plan | Price | Prompts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $95 a month | 50 |
| Pro | $245 a month | 150 |
| Advanced | $495 a month | 350 |
| Enterprise | Custom, billed yearly | Custom |

Prompts are checked daily, and every plan has unlimited users. Starter covers one country, Pro and Advanced three, Enterprise as many as you need. On Starter, Pro and Advanced you choose three models from eight: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Bing AI Mode, Naver AI Briefing and Baidu's ERNIE. Each extra model costs $30 a month on Starter. Claude, Grok, DeepSeek and the other API models are on Enterprise only, which tracks up to 26. Yearly billing takes 15% off, and there is a free trial.

## 1. AI Visibility Report: one look instead of a subscription

This one is ours. The [free check](/ai-visibility-report) finds the searches your customers make and asks the most searched one on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The full report is $29, once. It asks five searches on six engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok twice each, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode once), about 50 answers in all. It shows every answer word for word, who is named in your place and the pages the answers were built from, and ends in a fix kit. The same searches are asked again after 30 days.

It is not a tracker, and it does not cover Perplexity or Copilot.

**Pick it when:** you have never looked at what the engines say, or you want Claude and Grok in the answers without paying for Enterprise.

## 2. Otterly.AI: daily tracking for less

[Otterly's Lite plan](https://help.otterly.ai/pricing-of-otterlyai) is $29 a month for 16 prompts checked daily on four engines, or 21 on three. Per prompt on three engines, that is about $1.38 a month, against $1.90 on Peec's Starter plan. Standard is $189 a month and Premium $489. There is a free trial.

**Pick it when:** a short list of prompts every day is enough, and the price matters more than seats for a team.

## 3. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: inside Semrush

[Semrush's toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) is $99 a month per license, with 25 prompts checked daily, brand reports for one domain and AI checks in its site audit. Another 50 prompts are $60 a month. Semrush says there is no free trial for it.

**Pick it when:** your team already does its keyword research in Semrush and wants the AI numbers beside it.

## 4. Ahrefs Brand Radar: mentions at scale

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) sells Brand Radar from $199 a month. It measures how often a brand is mentioned across a very large set of AI answers, more than a list of your own prompts. Your own prompts are bought in packs, from $50 a month for 2,500 checks.

**Pick it when:** you want to compare brands across a whole market, not watch 50 chosen prompts.

## 5. AthenaHQ: more models, and a free plan

[AthenaHQ](https://athenahq.ai/plans) has a free plan with 300 credits, one credit per AI response. Starter is $295 a month for 3,600 credits and covers eleven models, among them ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Grok.

**Pick it when:** your customers use Claude, Grok or Copilot and you want them tracked on a plan you can buy online.

## 6. Profound: the enterprise option

[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) retired its self-serve plans in September 2026 and now sells one Enterprise plan at a custom price. Its pricing page lists up to ten answer engines, prompt volume data, analytics of visits by AI agents, single sign-on and SOC 2 compliance.

**Pick it when:** you are a large brand and those features are requirements.

## When Peec AI is still the right choice

- **A team logs in every week.** Every plan has unlimited users, so the price does not grow with the team.
- **You track 50 prompts or more.** Starter holds 50, against 16 to 21 on Otterly's Lite plan and 25 on Semrush's toolkit.
- **You sell in Korea or China.** Naver AI Briefing and ERNIE are among the models you can choose on Peec's ordinary plans.

## Questions

### Is there a free alternative to Peec AI?

For a first look, yes. AthenaHQ has a free plan with 300 credits, and our free check asks one of your customers' searches on three engines with no account. Otterly.AI and Peec itself have free trials. Nothing free tracks 50 prompts every day.

### What is cheaper than Peec AI?

Otterly.AI's Lite plan at $29 a month, for fewer prompts. If you do not need a daily number, a one-time report at $29 costs less than a third of one month of Peec.

### Does Peec AI track Claude and Grok?

Only on the Enterprise plan, according to its pricing page on 25 November 2026. The Starter, Pro and Advanced plans choose from ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Bing AI Mode, Naver AI Briefing and ERNIE.

### Peec AI or Otterly.AI?

Peec if a team needs 50 or more prompts and unlimited seats. Otterly if one person watches a short list and the monthly price comes first. Both have free trials: run the same ten prompts on each and compare.
