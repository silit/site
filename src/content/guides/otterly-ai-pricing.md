OtterlyAI (also written Otterly.AI) tracks what AI engines say about a brand: it asks your prompts every day and reports how often you are named and which sources are cited. It costs from $29 a month, and every plan is priced in credits.

Below is what each plan buys, how the credits turn into prompts, and five alternatives for when OtterlyAI is not the fit.

**A note on where we stand.** We make the first alternative on this list. What is written about OtterlyAI was read on its [pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing) and its [help page on plans](https://help.otterly.ai/pricing-of-otterlyai) on 26 November 2026, and about the others on their own pages between 23 and 26 November. Prices change, so check the page before you buy.

## OtterlyAI plans

| Plan | Price | Credits | Prompts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lite | $29 | 2,000 | 21 |
| Standard | $189 | 15,000 | 161 |
| Premium | $489 | 50,000 | 537 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | From 1,000 |

Prices and credits are per month, and the prompts are what each plan tracks on three engines, checked daily. The prompt counts are the help page's; the pricing page rounds them to 20, 160 and 535. Paying yearly takes 15% off. Prices are in US dollars or euros and exclude tax. There is a free trial for new users, but no free plan.

Every plan includes unlimited brands, competitors, team members, countries and languages. The plans differ in the rest:

- **Lite:** one workspace, three recommendations a week, daily tracking. No extra credits can be bought.
- **Standard:** unlimited workspaces and recommendations, API and MCP access with 2,000 requests a month each, and a Data Studio connector. Another 5,000 credits cost $65 a month.
- **Premium:** weekly tracking as an option, 5,000 API and MCP requests a month, and a personal onboarding. Another 10,000 credits cost $99 a month.
- **Enterprise:** from 1,000 prompts, single sign-on and a dedicated customer success manager.

## How the credits work

One credit is one prompt checked once on one engine. A month of daily checks is 31 checks, so one prompt on one engine uses 31 credits a month. Claude counts as 3 credits per check; the other six engines (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot) count as 1.

Credits are not a balance that runs down. They set how many prompts you can track at the same time, and you can move them between workspaces and engines.

That makes the engines you choose as important as the plan. Here is what Lite's 2,000 credits track, checked daily:

| Engines | Credits per prompt a month | Prompts on Lite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 engines, no Claude | 93 | 21 |
| 4 engines, no Claude | 124 | 16 |
| 2 engines and Claude | 155 | 12 |
| All 7 engines | 279 | 7 |

Weekly checks, about four a month, cover roughly seven times as many prompts, but only Premium and Enterprise offer them.

**What a prompt costs.** On three engines without Claude, a prompt tracked daily works out at about $1.38 a month on Lite, $1.17 on Standard and $0.91 on Premium. On Peec AI's Starter plan it is $1.90.

## How many prompts you need

OtterlyAI advises 50 to 100 prompts per topic or market. Its pricing page cites its own study: tracking 100 prompts daily for 30 days cut the swing in brand coverage by about 73% compared with tracking 10. Lite's 21 prompts are below that range, so on Lite, read the trend over weeks rather than day by day.

| Tool | Starts at | Pick it over OtterlyAI when |

### 1. AI Visibility Report: one look, no subscription

This one is ours. The [free check](/ai-visibility-report) finds the searches your customers make and asks the most searched one on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The full report is $29, once. It asks five searches on six engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok twice each, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode once), about 50 answers, and shows every answer word for word, who is named in your place, the pages the answers were built from and a fix kit. The same searches are asked again after 30 days.

It is not a tracker, and it does not cover Perplexity or Copilot. **Pick it when** you have not yet looked at what the engines say, or you need Grok in the answers.

### 2. Peec AI: 50 prompts for $95

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) starts at $95 a month for 50 prompts checked daily on three models of your choice, in one country, with unlimited users. Claude and Grok are on its Enterprise plan only. Our [Peec AI alternatives](/guides/peec-ai-alternatives/) guide has its plans in full. **Pick it when** 20 prompts are too few and 160 are more than you need.

### 3. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: inside Semrush

[Semrush's toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) is $99 a month per license, with 25 prompts checked daily and brand reports for one domain. Semrush says there is no free trial for it. **Pick it when** your keyword research already lives in Semrush.

### 4. AthenaHQ: the most models on a public plan

[AthenaHQ](https://athenahq.ai/plans) has a free plan with 300 credits on five models. Its Starter plan is $295 a month for 3,600 AI responses on eleven models: ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI and Mistral. **Pick it when** your customers use the models OtterlyAI does not list.

### 5. Ahrefs Brand Radar: a whole market

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) sells Brand Radar from $199 a month. It measures how often brands are mentioned across a very large set of AI answers, beyond your own prompts. **Pick it when** you want to compare brands across a market rather than watch a list of prompts.

## When OtterlyAI is the right choice

- **You want daily tracking at the lowest monthly price.** $29 for 21 prompts on three engines.
- **You work for several brands.** Brands, competitors, countries and seats are unlimited on every plan, and Standard adds unlimited workspaces.
- **You want Claude on an ordinary plan.** It costs three credits a check, but it is available from Lite up.

## Questions

### How much does OtterlyAI cost?

Lite is $29 a month, Standard $189 and Premium $489, with 15% off for paying yearly. Enterprise is priced on request and starts from 1,000 prompts. Prices exclude tax.

### Does OtterlyAI have a free plan?

No. It has a free trial for new users. For a free first look at what AI says about your business, our free check needs no account.

### Why does Claude use more credits on OtterlyAI?

OtterlyAI says analysing Claude costs it three times as much, so each Claude answer counts as 3 credits. Adding Claude as one of three engines cuts Lite from 21 prompts to 12.

### What is the cheapest alternative to OtterlyAI?

Among the trackers we compared, none costs less a month than OtterlyAI's Lite plan. For a single look rather than a daily number, our report is $29 once, and AthenaHQ's free plan has 300 credits.
