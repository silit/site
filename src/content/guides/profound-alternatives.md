## First question: do you need a tracker yet?

Profound is a tracker. It asks the same prompts every day and draws the trend. That is worth paying for once you are changing things every week and want to see whether they worked.

If you have never looked at what the AI engines say about you, a tracker's first month mostly tells you one thing, every day: you are not named. A single careful look tells you the same and costs less. It is the difference between [a checker, an audit and a tracker](/tools/ai-search-audit/), and for a small business the audit usually comes first.

## The six alternatives

### 1. AI Visibility Report: one payment, no subscription

This one is ours. You type a website and the [free check](/ai-visibility-report) finds the searches your customers make and puts one of them to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The full report costs $29 once. It asks the five most searched on six engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok twice each, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode once), shows every answer word for word, counts who is named in your place, lists the pages the answers were built from, and ends in a fix kit with the text and code to paste. The same searches are asked again after 30 days.

What it is not: a tracker. There is no dashboard and no daily trend, and it does not cover Perplexity or Copilot. On a Webflow site, our app [AI SEO Deck](/webflow-apps/ai-seo-deck/) does the tracking: free for one site with three keywords checked monthly, and weekly from $19 a month.

**Good for:** a first look, a small business, an agency that wants a report for a client without another seat to pay for.

### 2. Otterly.AI: the lowest monthly price

[Otterly's Lite plan](https://webflow.otterly.ai/pricing) is $29 a month. Its [help page on plans](https://help.otterly.ai/pricing-of-otterlyai), updated on 10 November 2026, gives Lite 2,000 credits a month: enough for 16 prompts checked daily on four engines, or 21 on three. Standard is $189 a month and Premium $489, with room for roughly 120 and 400 prompts. There is a free trial.

**Good for:** watching a short list of prompts every day at the lowest monthly cost.

### 3. Peec AI: built for marketing teams

[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing) starts at $95 a month for 50 prompts on three models of your choice, checked daily, in one country, with unlimited users. Each extra model is $30 a month on that plan. Pro is $245 for 150 prompts and Advanced $495 for 350. There is a free trial.

**Good for:** a team that wants daily data on a real list of prompts and will log in every week.

### 4. Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit: if you already use Semrush

[Semrush's toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) is $99 a month per license. That includes 25 tracked prompts, checked daily, brand reports for one domain and AI checks in its site audit. Another 50 prompts are $60 a month and another domain $99 a month. Semrush's help says there is no free trial for it.

**Good for:** teams that live in Semrush and want AI numbers beside their keyword data.

### 5. Ahrefs Brand Radar: brand data at scale

[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/pricing) sells Brand Radar on its own from $199 a month. Tracking your own prompts is priced in packs of checks, from $50 a month for 2,500, where one prompt on one platform in one place is one check. Its ordinary plans, from $129 a month, include a small number of checks.

**Good for:** larger brands that want to see how often they are mentioned across a very large set of AI answers, more than a short list of their own prompts.

### 6. AthenaHQ: a free plan to try

[AthenaHQ](https://athenahq.ai/plans) has a free plan with 300 credits, where one credit is one AI response. The Starter plan is $295 a month for 3,600 credits and covers eleven models, among them ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and Grok.

**Good for:** trying a full platform at no cost before deciding, and teams that need many models in one place.

## What you give up by leaving Profound

It is fair to say what the cheaper tools do not match. Profound's Enterprise plan tracks up to ten answer engines every day, and its pricing page lists prompt volume data, analytics of visits by AI agents with integrations for hosting networks such as Cloudflare and Akamai, single sign-on and SOC 2 compliance. If you need those, ask for the demo. The alternatives here are for the teams that plan was not built for.

## How to choose

- **You have never checked what AI says about you:** start with a one-time look. Ours is $29, and the first search is free.
- **You want a number every day at the lowest price:** Otterly.AI Lite.
- **You have a marketing team and a list of 50 prompts or more:** Peec AI.
- **You already pay for an SEO suite:** the Semrush toolkit or Ahrefs Brand Radar, whichever you use.
- **You want to try a platform for free first:** AthenaHQ's free plan.
- **Your site is on Webflow:** AI SEO Deck checks three keywords a month free.

## Questions

### Is there a free alternative to Profound?

For a first look, yes. AthenaHQ has a free plan with 300 credits. Our free check asks one search on three engines with no account. Otterly.AI and Peec AI have free trials. Nothing free tracks a long list of prompts every day.

### What is the cheapest Profound alternative?

At $29, either a one-time report or a month of Otterly.AI Lite. Which is cheaper depends on whether you need the look once or the number every day.

### Do I need a tracker to improve my AI visibility?

No. You need to know what the answers say, which pages they were built from and what to fix. A one-time [audit](/guides/geo-audit/) gives you that, by hand or with a tool. A tracker tells you afterwards whether the fixes worked.
