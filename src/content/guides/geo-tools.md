GEO tools are the tools for generative engine optimization: getting a business named and cited in the answers of ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode. The same tools are sold as LLM SEO tools, AEO tools and AI visibility tools.

Most lists of GEO tools are lists of trackers, the subscriptions that ask the same prompts every day and draw the trend. Tracking is one of four jobs, and the one to pay for last. Two of the others have free tools from Google, Microsoft and Cloudflare.

1. **Let AI read your site:** check that AI crawlers are allowed in and find your words in the page code.
2. **See what AI answers today:** one look at the answers for your customers' searches.
3. **Track the answers over time:** the same prompts every day or week.
4. **Count what AI sends you:** how often your pages are shown or cited, and the visits that follow.

## Job 1: let AI read your site

An engine can only name you from pages it can read. Most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, and a single line in robots.txt can shut them out. These tools check both.

### Cloudflare AI Crawl Control

If your site runs through Cloudflare, [AI Crawl Control](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/get-started/) lists the AI crawlers that visit it, and lets you block any one of them. It is on the free plan, with two limits: it recognises crawlers by the name they announce, and its metrics cover only the past 24 hours.

**Good for:** seeing whether OpenAI's, Anthropic's and other crawlers come at all, and whether something is blocking them.

### AEO checker and llms.txt checker

Our [AEO checker](/tools/aeo-checker/) reads your robots.txt and your home page the way an AI crawler does, without running scripts, and shows what is left. The [llms.txt checker](/tools/llms-txt-checker/) checks an llms.txt file against the format and reads the robots.txt rules for each AI crawler. Both are free with no sign-up.

**Good for:** the two settings that keep a site out of every answer, checked in a minute.

### Schema Markup Validator

Schema.org's [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org/) shows the structured data a page carries and flags errors. Use it to check your Organization markup: name, address, logo and the profiles that are yours. Google says its AI features need [no special schema](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), so there is nothing more to add for them.

**Good for:** making sure what your markup says matches what your page says.

## Job 2: see what AI answers today

A one-time check answers the first question, are you named at all. Three of these are free from SEO companies, and they differ in what they ask and what they show.

### Semrush AI Search Visibility Checker

[Semrush's free checker](https://www.semrush.com/free-tools/ai-search-visibility-checker/) needs no account and allows three runs a day. It covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and gives an AI Visibility Score from 0 to 100, a count of mentions, a count of citations and the pages of your site that are cited most.

**Good for:** a quick score, and the pages AI already cites.

### Ahrefs AI Visibility Checker

[Ahrefs' free checker](https://www.ahrefs.com/ai-visibility-checker) needs no sign-up and covers six platforms: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, AI Overviews and AI Mode. It shows your mentions by platform and the top five topics, cited domains and cited pages. Ahrefs calls the free report a limited preview of its paid Brand Radar.

**Good for:** the widest list of platforms in a free check, and the domains cited beside you.

### HubSpot AI Search Grader

[HubSpot's grader](https://www.hubspot.com/ai-search-grader) is a free one-time check on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It scores a brand out of 100 across five parts: sentiment, presence quality, brand recognition, share of voice and market competition. The full report sits behind a form.

**Good for:** how AI describes a brand you name, and the tone it uses.

### AI search audit

Ours starts from the other end. You type a website, and the [free first pass](/tools/ai-search-audit/) finds the searches your customers make, with how often each is made on Google, asks the most searched one on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, and shows each answer word for word with every business in it marked. The question is the search a customer makes, so the answer shows who that customer is sent to.

The [full report](/ai-visibility-report) is $29, once. It asks five searches on six engines, about 50 answers, lists the pages the answers were built from and ends in a fix kit. The same searches are asked again after 30 days.

**Good for:** reading the actual answers a customer gets, with the names and the sources in them.

## Job 3: track the answers over time

A tracker asks a fixed list of prompts every day or week and draws the trend. It is worth paying for once you are changing things often and want to see whether the changes worked. Our [comparison of AI visibility tools](/guides/profound-alternatives/) has the plans in detail; here is the short version.

- **[Otterly.AI](https://help.otterly.ai/pricing-of-otterlyai):** the lowest monthly price. Lite is $29 a month for 16 prompts checked daily on four engines, or 21 on three. Free trial.
- **[Peec AI](https://peec.ai/pricing):** $95 a month for 50 prompts on three models of your choice, checked daily, with unlimited users. Free trial.
- **[Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit):** $99 a month for 25 prompts checked daily, beside Semrush's keyword data. No free trial.
- **[Ahrefs Brand Radar](https://ahrefs.com/pricing):** from $199 a month, built to measure mentions across a very large set of AI answers. Your own prompts are bought in packs, from $50 a month.
- **[AthenaHQ](https://athenahq.ai/plans):** a free plan with 300 credits, one credit per AI response; Starter is $295 a month and covers eleven models.
- **[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing):** since September 2026, Enterprise only, at a price you get after a demo call.
- **[AI SEO Deck](/webflow-apps/ai-seo-deck/):** ours, for Webflow sites. Free for one site with three keywords checked monthly; weekly checks from $19 a month.

Before you pay for one, check two things on its plan page: which engines are included at your price, and whether it shows the sources behind each answer. The sources are what you act on.

## Job 4: count what AI sends you

The trackers ask their own prompts. These three count what happens to your site in the real thing, and all three are free.

### Google Search Console

The [Generative AI performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139) counts how many times links to your site were shown in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, date and device. It counts impressions only, with no queries, and it appears once a site has enough of them. Google rolled it out to all websites worldwide by 31 August 2026.

**Good for:** the only count of your links in Google's AI answers that comes from Google.

### Bing Webmaster Tools

Bing's [AI Performance report](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview), in public preview since February 2026, counts how often your pages are cited as sources in Microsoft Copilot, in the AI summaries in Bing and in some partner products. It shows total citations, citations per page and a sample of the grounding queries: the phrases the AI searched with when it retrieved your page. In June 2026 Bing [added](https://blogs.bing.com/search/June-2026/New-AI-Visibility-Insights-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Intents-Topics-Citation-Share-Compare) intents, topics, a comparison with an earlier period and your share of all citations for a query, without the names of the other sites.

**Good for:** the searches behind your citations in Microsoft's AI answers.

### Google Analytics

Visits from ChatGPT's answers carry `utm_source=chatgpt.com`, which OpenAI's [publisher FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq) says it adds to the links it sends, so you can filter them in Google Analytics or any other analytics tool. Clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode come from google.com like any other Google search, so analytics cannot separate them; the Search Console report above is the count for those two.

**Good for:** knowing whether AI answers bring visits, and to which pages.

## Which ones a small business needs

- **Start free.** Add your site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, run the AEO checker, and do one free check for your customers' main search.
- **Keep a sheet.** Five searches, asked once a month, are enough to see a trend. The [step-by-step plan](/guides/ai-search-optimization/) shows what to do between checks.
- **Pay for one careful look** when you want every answer, the pages behind them and the fixes in one place: a [GEO audit](/guides/geo-audit/) by hand, or our report for $29.
- **Pay for a tracker** once you are changing things every week. Below that pace, a monthly sheet tells you the same.

## What GEO tools cannot do

- **Put you in an answer.** No tool controls what an engine writes. They show where you stand and why; the change happens on your pages and on the pages the engines read.
- **Give a score that travels.** Every score out of 100 is that company's own formula. Compare a tool with itself over time, not with another tool.
- **Promise a steady answer.** The same question asked twice often gets different sources. A 2026 study of ChatGPT's search, covered in our guide to [getting cited by ChatGPT](/guides/how-to-get-cited-by-chatgpt/), found that 41 to 45% of the pages it gathered turned up in only one run. One check is a sample.
- **Measure AI search volume.** The AI companies do not publish how often a question is asked, so treat any "AI search volume" figure as an estimate.

## Questions

### What are GEO tools?

Software for generative engine optimization: checking whether AI crawlers can read a site, what AI engines answer about a business, how that changes over time and how often the engines cite the site. Many of them call themselves AI visibility tools.

### Are GEO tools and LLM SEO tools the same thing?

Yes. GEO, AEO, LLM SEO and AI visibility are names for the same work, and the tools sold under each name do the same four jobs. Our guide to [LLM visibility](/guides/llm-visibility/) explains how that visibility is measured.

### Are there free GEO tools?

Yes, for every job. Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools count your AI impressions and citations, Cloudflare shows which AI crawlers visit, and Semrush, Ahrefs, HubSpot and we offer free checks of what AI answers. Free tracking is limited: AthenaHQ's free plan has 300 credits, and AI SEO Deck checks three keywords a month on one Webflow site.

### What is the best GEO tool for a small business?

The free ones first: Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and one free check. After that, a one-time report if you need the sources and the fixes, or Otterly.AI at $29 a month if you need a number every day.

### Do I need a paid GEO tool at all?

No. A spreadsheet, a private browser window and a few hours a month cover the same ground by hand. Tools save the asking and counting.
